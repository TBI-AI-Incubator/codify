# AKN interoperability scope

Codify targets Akoma Ntoso 3.0 and provides schema validation. Its internal
choices, including inline annex content, may differ from AKN4EU profiles and
individual importers. This tree does not include a complete AKN4EU conformance
suite or a certification of compatibility with Indigo or other products.

Validate each intended interchange workflow with representative documents and
the receiving system. Structural validity does not establish source fidelity.
